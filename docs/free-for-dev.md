# Free for Dev (`free-for.dev`)

[Free for Dev](https://free-for.dev) (hosted on GitHub at [ripienaar/free-for-dev](https://github.com/ripienaar/free-for-dev)) is a massive, community-curated list of software (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, DBaaS, APIs, CI/CD, hosting, monitoring, testing, design tools, etc.) that offer free tiers for developers.

---

## Purpose & Scope

Developers, startups, and open-source projects often need cloud infrastructure, databases, monitoring, authentication, and hosting without incurring initial costs. `free-for.dev` aggregates over 700+ free services across 35+ categories so you can build, host, and scale applications for free.

---

## Major Service Categories

- **Hosting & Compute (PaaS/IaaS):** Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages/Workers, Render, Fly.io, Railway, AWS Free Tier, GCP Free Tier, Oracle Cloud Free Tier.
- **Database as a Service (DBaaS):** Supabase (PostgreSQL), PlanetScale, MongoDB Atlas, Neon (Serverless Postgres), Turso (SQLite/libsql), Upstash (Redis).
- **Authentication & Security:** Auth0, Clerk, Firebase Auth, Stytch, Supabase Auth.
- **APIs & Backend Utilities:** Resend, SendGrid, Postmark, Twilio, Cloudinary, Uploadthing.
- **CI/CD & Code Quality:** GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, CircleCI, Codecov, SonarCloud.
- **Monitoring & Observability:** Sentry, Better Stack, Logtail, Datadog (Free tier), Axiom.
- **AI & Machine Learning:** Hugging Face Inference API, Groq Free Tier, Together AI, Replicate.

---

## Guidelines for Inclusion

1. **Must offer a genuine free tier:** Not just a temporary 14-day or 30-day free trial.
2. **Developer focused:** Must be useful to developers, sysadmins, or DevOps engineers.
3. **No credit card required (preferred):** Services that do not require credit card activation are explicitly highlighted.
